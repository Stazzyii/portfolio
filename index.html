About me

Technology that works — for people and for business.

I'm a full-stack web developer who enjoys turning ideas into websites and applications that are fast, clear and genuinely useful.

My work sits where three disciplines meet. As a web developer, I design and build responsive websites and web applications, from the interface people see to the logic and data behind it. As a data entry professional, I've learned that accuracy, organisation and confidentiality are not optional, and that care shapes how I write code and handle information. Through IT work at Deacons Publishers, I've seen first-hand how much businesses depend on technology that simply works.

I'm especially interested in helping businesses build a strong, trustworthy online presence and in solving practical problems with clean, maintainable software. I'm always learning, and I care about the details that make a website feel professional: performance, accessibility and a design that works on every screen.
